Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to anshidck.com. This website is the personal portfolio and digital marketing consultancy website operated by Mohammad Anshid Ck ("I", "me", or "my").

This Privacy Policy explains in transparent, practical terms how personal information is handled when you browse my portfolio, read articles on the blog, schedule a strategy consultation, or submit an inquiry through the contact form or quote estimator.

Core Commitment: I respect your privacy. I do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal contact details to data brokers or third-party marketing networks.

2. Information Collected

Depending on how you interact with this website, the following categories of information may be collected:

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you submit an inquiry through the contact form, you provide your name, email address, phone number, areas of interest (e.g., SEO, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Web Design, AEO), and message content.
  • Quote Builder & Project Estimator: When using the interactive quote estimator, you may submit project specifications, budget preferences, timeline goals, company name, and contact details.
  • Direct Inquiries: When reaching out directly via email (anshidchelekodan@gmail.com) or WhatsApp (+91 97781 79727), I receive your contact identifier and message history.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical & Log Data: When you access the site, standard server and browser request data may be logged, including your IP address, browser type and version, device category, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps.
  • Aggregated Usage Analytics: Anonymized interaction metrics (such as button clicks, page scroll depth, and reading duration) processed through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information submitted to this website is processed strictly for legitimate professional purposes, including:

  • Responding to your business inquiries, consultation requests, and service questions.
  • Preparing customized proposals, digital marketing audits, and quotation estimates.
  • Communicating with you regarding active or prospective client projects.
  • Analyzing aggregate traffic patterns to improve website performance, readability, and content relevance.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing spam or abusive automated submissions.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

This website is built as a static site and integrates a limited number of reputable third-party services to operate efficiently:

  • GitHub Pages (Hosting): Hosted on GitHub Pages (operated by GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft Corporation). GitHub may process technical access logs for security and infrastructure diagnostics in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Tag Manager & Google Analytics 4: Used to collect aggregated, non-sensitive telemetry data regarding website usage. These services use first-party cookies to measure user interactions. IP anonymization is applied by default in GA4.
  • Google Apps Script & Google Sheets (Form Processing): Form submissions on this site are securely transmitted via HTTPS to a private Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in private Google Drive spreadsheets accessible only by me for inquiry follow-up.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.): The website contains voluntary links and a floating button allowing visitors to initiate direct WhatsApp conversations. Engaging via WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy terms.
  • Content Delivery Networks & Fonts: Google Fonts and Cloudflare / CDNJS are used to serve typography (Montserrat) and iconography (FontAwesome) reliably and efficiently.

5. Cookies & Browser Tracking

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. This website uses minimal cookies primarily for:

  • Analytical Cookies: Measuring aggregated traffic and performance via Google Analytics 4 to understand which blog topics and portfolio case studies are most helpful to readers.
  • Functional Storage: Storing lightweight client preferences (such as recording that you viewed the latest blog badge) in your browser's local storage.

You can choose to disable or block cookies through your individual browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to browse the website or contact me.

6. Data Retention & Security

Inquiry records and project communications are retained only as long as necessary to complete client discussions, fulfill agreed business services, or comply with standard record-keeping obligations. Inactive inquiries are periodically reviewed and deleted.

All traffic to anshidck.com is served over encrypted HTTPS (TLS) connections. While reasonable administrative and technical precautions are maintained to protect incoming submissions, please recognize that no method of electronic internet transmission is completely invulnerable.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to request confirmation of whether I hold any personal data regarding your inquiries.
  • The right to request a correction of inaccurate or outdated contact information.
  • The right to request the deletion of your contact inquiry details from my records.
  • The right to opt out of any future direct business communications at any time.

To exercise any of these choices, simply send an email to anshidchelekodan@gmail.com with your request.

8. Children's Privacy

This website provides professional business-to-business and digital marketing services. It is not intended for or directed toward individuals under the age of 18, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
